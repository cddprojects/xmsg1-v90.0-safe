Glossary

Job-ad terms, explained.

Base pay
The fixed portion of your earnings, before commission, bonuses or allowances.
Commission-only
Your earnings rest wholly on sales or results, with no fixed amount underneath.
Ad hoc
Occasional or one-off work, with no ongoing obligation for either side.
Retainer
A recurring fee paid to a freelancer so they stay available for an agreed amount of work.
Flexi-hours
Start and finish times that can move, usually around a set block of core hours.
FWA
Flexible work arrangement. It covers flexi-time, flexi-place and flexi-load.
Core hours
The stretch of the day when colleagues expect you to be reachable, even in flexible or remote roles.
Hybrid
A pattern that divides the week between the office and another place, often home.
Freelancer
Someone who is self-employed and paid per project or task, and who manages their own tax and contributions.
Platform worker
A person who gets work through an app or website, as with ride-hailing and delivery.
Gig
A short job built around one task.
Contract / temp
Employment for a fixed period, sometimes arranged through an agency.
Probation
A trial period at the start of a job, when each side sees if it works.
Notice period
How much warning you or the employer must give before the job ends.
Training bond
An agreement to stay for a set time after training, or to repay its cost if you leave.
PMET
Professionals, Managers, Executives and Technicians.
Money mule
Someone whose bank account is used to pass on scam proceeds, often without knowing it. Doing so is a criminal offence.
