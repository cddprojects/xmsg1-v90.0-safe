Roles in Demand in Singapore and the Skills Behind Them

Last reviewed November 2026

Titles change from year to year, yet the skills employers want fall into a handful of familiar groups.

Carenursing, allied health, eldercare
Dataanalysts, engineers, AI support
Digitalproduct, marketing, cybersecurity
Opslogistics, projects, compliance

Care and health

Manpower plans keep returning to nurses, therapists, care associates and community support workers.

Data and technology

There is a need for data analysts, software engineers and cloud and security specialists, and a good share of this work can be done remotely or on contract.

Digital marketing and content

Performance marketing, SEO, content creation and design are the main skills. Freelance and project work are popular ways to get started.

Finance, compliance and operations

Jobs in risk, audit, project coordination and supply chain stay steady across many industries.

Making use of this list

  • See what recent MyCareersFuture listings actually pay.
  • Pay attention to the skills required, not just the job title.
  • Find out if part-time, hybrid or contract versions are offered.

Want to know what this kind of work looks like day to day? Our job types overview explains.

Common questions

Can sought-after roles be done part-time or remotely?

Some can, especially analysis, content, support and project work. The employer is the best person to ask.

Is a degree always needed?

Not always. Many employers now name skills and certifications next to a degree, or in place of one.

Key takeaways

  1. Demand clusters around care, data, digital work, finance and operations.
  2. Many of these roles also exist in part-time, contract or remote form.
  3. Salaries vary a great deal, so compare recent listings with official pay data.
  4. A short course can open the door to a neighbouring role.
