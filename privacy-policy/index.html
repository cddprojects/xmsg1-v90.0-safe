Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This website is published as FlexiBrowse. This policy describes what personal data we handle when you visit or write to us, and why, in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (the "PDPA").

Using the site or sending us your details means you accept the practices set out below.

1. About this website

FlexiBrowse is an independent site with articles and tools on careers, industries and skills in Singapore. It is not an employment agency. It does not list vacancies and does not recruit, screen or place candidates.

2. What we collect

We hold personal data only if you send it to us yourself, through the contact form or by email. That can include:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • whatever you write in your message.

Please do not include your NRIC number, Singpass details, OTPs, bank details or passwords. We will never ask for any of them.

The hours planner works inside your browser. Whatever you type there stays on your device and is never sent to us.

3. Server and technical records

Our hosting service may log routine technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you open. These records help keep the site secure and running.

4. Why we use your data

Your personal data is used only to:

  • answer your question, comment or correction;
  • weigh up a topic you propose;
  • keep the site safe and working;
  • comply with the law.

We will not send you marketing without your consent, and we do not sell personal data.

5. Who may receive it

We pass personal data on only in these cases:

  • to providers that host the site or handle our email, and who are bound to look after it;
  • when the law requires or permits it, such as a court order or a request from a regulator or the authorities;
  • when you have agreed.

6. Data held overseas

A provider may store or process data outside Singapore. If so, we take reasonable steps to make sure it is protected to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

7. Security and how long we keep data

We take reasonable precautions against unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, copying, changes or disposal of the data we hold. Because nothing sent over the internet is entirely safe, we can't promise complete security.

Data is kept only while it is needed for the purposes in section 4, or where the law says we must keep it. After that we delete it or make it anonymous.

8. Your choices and rights

Under the PDPA, you can:

  • request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and be told how it has been used or disclosed over the past year;
  • ask for mistakes or gaps in it to be corrected;
  • withdraw consent to how we collect, use or disclose it.

Send any request to our Data Protection Officer using the details below. We may ask you to prove who you are and will answer within a reasonable time. If you withdraw consent, we will tell you what that means, and we might not be able to carry on with your enquiry.

9. Links to other sites

Pages here point to websites run by other organisations, including government agencies. Their privacy practices are their own, so read their policies before you share your data with them.

10. Younger visitors

The site is meant for a general adult audience, not for children. If you think a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

11. Updates

This policy may be revised from time to time. The current version, with its date, is always on this page.

12. Data Protection Officer
