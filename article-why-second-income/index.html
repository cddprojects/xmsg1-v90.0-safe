Side income

Why Side Income Is on the Rise in Singapore

Last reviewed November 2026

Growing numbers of people are taking a part-time role next to their main job, or picking up freelance projects at weekends. The reasons are down to earth.

<35 hrscontracted hours a week for part-time work

1income that seldom covers every cost

Costs drive the trend

Rent, childcare, healthcare and daily spending all put pressure on households. Extra income gives some breathing space and leaves your main career untouched.

More flexible options exist now

Part-time listings, home-based roles, online work and freelance projects make it simpler to slot extra work into your week. Most of it is ordinary paid work done at home or online, with pay that follows the role and the hours.

A side role can be a low-risk experiment

It is also a cheap way to try a new field, build up a portfolio or ease back in after a break.

Sort these out before you start

  • How many hours you can really give each week.
  • How tax and CPF apply, depending on whether you are employed or self-employed.
  • Whether your main contract permits other work.

Our guide to working from home compares the options.

Common questions

How much time does a side income take?

That depends on your goal and your hourly rate. Our hours planner gives a rough idea.

Should I tell my main employer?

Look at your employment contract, since some require approval for work outside the job.

Key takeaways

  1. A part-time role adds income without you giving up your main job.
  2. Home-based and freelance work can save commuting time.
  3. Side income works best when hours, tax and CPF are clear from day one.
  4. Treat online earnings claims with caution, because pay follows the work done.
