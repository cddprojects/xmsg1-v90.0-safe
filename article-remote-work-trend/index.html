Flexible Work in Singapore: How Remote and Hybrid Arrangements Became Normal

Last reviewed November 2026

What used to be a perk is now a routine topic between employers and staff.

2024when the national flexible work guidelines took effect
2 monthshow long an employer has to reply to a formal request
3forms: flexi-place, flexi-time, flexi-load

The shift

Employers are now expected to have a process for weighing flexible work requests. Approval isn't guaranteed, but you now have a clear route for asking.

How remote and hybrid look in practice

Some remote roles are fully remote, while others require a fixed number of days in the office. Working from home in Singapore normally means agreed hours with a local employer.

What employers want to see

They look for clear communication, dependable results and a suitable place to work. Proposing a short trial with a review date often helps.

Thinking of asking? Read how to submit a flexible work request.

Common questions

May I ask to work from home?

Yes, by submitting a formal flexible work arrangement request. Our step-by-step guide shows how.

Can Singapore residents take remote roles?

Many can. Check which time zone, employing entity and payroll apply.

Key takeaways

  1. Requests for flexible work now have a place in national guidelines.
  2. Remote listings run from fully remote to hybrid.
  3. Working from home is normally agreed as a set of hours and a place.
  4. An employer may still say no, but should explain why.
