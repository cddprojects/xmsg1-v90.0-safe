About Us

Information on flexible work in Singapore.

Who this is for

FlexiBrowse is an independent publication about careers, industry trends and skills in Singapore. It is written for anyone planning a next move, or weighing up part-time, home-based, online or freelance work as a source of extra income. We are not connected with any government agency.

You have limited time, real bills and little patience for jargon. So each guide explains what the work involves, what a typical week looks like, and what to check before you agree to anything.

How we write our guides

Written for job seekers

Every guide answers the questions people actually ask: hours, how pay is worked out, CPF, contracts and how to begin.

Based on official sources

We link to MOM, CPF Board, IRAS and MyCareersFuture so you can confirm the details yourself.

Reviewed and dated

Each guide shows when it was last reviewed, so you can judge how current it is.
