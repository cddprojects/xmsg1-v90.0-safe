Working from home

Working from home in Singapore: the main routes compared

Most people who want to earn from home in Singapore pick one of four routes: a part-time job, a home-based role, online work or freelancing. Each one suits a different mix of hours, skills and need for stability.

Before you say yes

Points worth checking.

Realistic expectations

Earnings depend on the role, your skills and your hours. Treat any offer of high pay for little effort with suspicion.

Costs

Allow for transport, equipment, internet and any tax on what you earn.

Your rights

Employees have contracts, CPF and leave, while freelancers look after their own tax and contributions.

Balance

Extra income shouldn't leave you exhausted, so build in time to rest.

Questions to ask

  • How many hours can I give each week?
  • Do I need a steady income, or flexibility?
  • Is this employment or self-employment?
  • What costs will I have?
  • How will I keep track of income for tax?
  • Does my main job allow outside work?

Mistakes to avoid

  • Chasing offers that promise easy money
  • Overlooking costs and tax
  • Committing to more hours than you can keep up
  • Leaving the terms unwritten

FAQ

Common questions.

Is there a single best way to earn from home in Singapore?

No, it depends on your skills and available hours. Part-time and home-based roles give steady hours, while freelancing gives you more control.

Can I begin without experience?

Some entry-level roles, such as support or admin, don't ask for much experience, though employers differ.

Do I have to declare money earned at home?

Yes. Income from employment and freelance work is taxable. Check with IRAS.
