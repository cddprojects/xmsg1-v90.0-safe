Work and Caregiving: Weighing Costs and Choices for Families

Last reviewed November 2026

Many parents and caregivers aren't asking whether to work. They are asking whether the pay and the hours genuinely add up.

Begin with the hours you really have

Note down what you can truly give each week, allowing for the school run, meals and appointments. A role that fits those hours beats a better rate that doesn't.

Bring up flexibility early

  • Are the hours fixed, or can you pick your slots?
  • Can part of the work happen at home?
  • What is the plan if a child or parent is unwell?

Work out the real numbers

Take childcare, transport and other work-related costs away from the pay you expect. A few hours in a good fit can be worth more than many hours in a stressful job.

Go easy on yourself

Working part-time, or pausing work, is not a failure. You can change your mind as your family's needs change.

Our job types guide covers roles with flexible hours.

Common questions

Do any jobs fit around school hours?

Some part-time and home-based roles have hours that work around school. Ask about flexibility early on.

Could my current employer change my hours?

You can submit a formal flexible work request. Our guide explains the process.

Key takeaways

  1. Begin with the hours you can honestly commit.
  2. Raise flexibility before you accept a role.
  3. Take childcare and work costs off the pay you expect.
  4. Your decision can be revisited as life changes.
