Work from home

Work-from-home roles in Singapore: what they involve and how to start

Most home-based jobs in Singapore are service or admin roles with fixed hours. A good one and a poor one tend to differ in the details: who supplies equipment, how closely you are monitored and who covers which costs.

Getting started

5 steps.

  1. 1

    Create a workable space

    You need a quiet corner, dependable internet and a laptop. Ask whether the employer supplies equipment.

  2. 2

    Take stock of your skills

    Think about communication, accuracy and the tools you already handle, such as email, spreadsheets and chat apps.

  3. 3

    Search official channels

    Use MyCareersFuture and the careers pages of employers you have checked. Find out whether the role is employment or freelance.

  4. 4

    Clarify what is expected

    Ask about reply times, core hours, tools, supervision and how performance is judged.

  5. 5

    Get the terms in writing

    Hours, pay, CPF, equipment and notice period should all be written down.

Before you say yes

Points worth checking.

Equipment and costs

Find out who provides the laptop and software, and whether you are expected to pay for your own internet or electricity.

Monitoring and availability

Some roles log activity or expect quick replies. Ask how performance is measured and what response times apply.

Employee or freelance

The job title won't say. The answer decides CPF, leave and who deals with tax, so ask directly.

Customer data

If you handle customer information, expect company rules on devices and storage, in line with the PDPA.

Questions to ask

  • Is this employment, or freelance work?
  • What are the core hours, and how is time recorded?
  • Who supplies the equipment and software?
  • How is pay worked out, and when does it arrive?
  • Is training paid?
  • Can the hours change, and with how much notice?

Mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming that home-based means no set hours
  • Not finding out whether CPF applies
  • Handling company data on a shared or unsecured device
  • Leaving the terms unwritten
FAQ

Common questions.

Are home-based jobs in Singapore genuine?

Yes. Many employers recruit for support, admin and content roles that can be done at home. Check the employer and the terms just as you would for any job.

How does working from home differ from remote work?

Working from home usually means fixed hours from your own home. Remote work can mean any location, and sometimes an overseas team. Our remote jobs guide explains more.

Will I need my own laptop?

That depends on the employer, so ask before you accept.
