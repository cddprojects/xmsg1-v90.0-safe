Remote roles

Remote jobs in Singapore: skills, employers and what to clarify

Remote roles usually reward specialist skills, and plenty are with overseas teams. If you live in Singapore, the questions that matter are practical ones: who your employer is, how your hours overlap with theirs, and how tax and CPF will work.

Getting started

5 steps.

  1. 1

    Let your work speak

    Assemble a small portfolio or a few work samples. Remote employers cannot watch you work, so evidence of results counts.

  2. 2

    Aim at suitable roles

    Search by skill, and look for listings that are open to people based in Singapore.

  3. 3

    Find out who the employer is

    Confirm the legal employer, its location and the way you will be paid.

  4. 4

    Get ready for video interviews

    Test your camera, microphone and connection, and have examples of independent work to hand.

  5. 5

    Ask about tax and CPF

    Find out how employment, tax and CPF will be handled for your circumstances.

Before you say yes

Points worth checking.

Employing entity

CPF is linked to employment by a Singapore employer. If your employer is overseas, or you work as a contractor, the position can be different. Check with CPF Board and IRAS.

Time zone

Find out which hours you must be online and how much room there is to adjust them.

Pay

Confirm the currency, how you will be paid and when, plus any conversion or bank charges.

Tax

Money from an overseas employer is still income. Ask IRAS how it applies to you.

Questions to ask

  • Who is my legal employer, and where are they based?
  • Which time zone and core hours apply?
  • Am I an employee or a contractor?
  • Which currency and payment method will be used?
  • What equipment and tools will I be given?
  • How will tax and CPF be dealt with?

Mistakes to avoid

  • Overlooking how far the time zones overlap
  • Assuming CPF comes with an overseas contractor role
  • Failing to keep income records for tax
  • Leaving the terms unwritten
FAQ

Common questions.

Is remote work only for people in tech?

No. Marketing, design, support, project management and content roles are often remote too.

Must I be in Singapore to be hired?

Some employers require it for tax or legal reasons and others don't. Raise it early.

Is income from an overseas employer taxable?

How it is treated depends on your situation. Ask IRAS or a tax professional.
