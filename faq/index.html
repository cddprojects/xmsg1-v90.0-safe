FAQ

Quick answers.

Is FlexiBrowse a job board?

No. We write about kinds of work and how they operate in Singapore. To see live vacancies, go to MyCareersFuture or an employer's own careers page.

Is there a charge for FlexiBrowse?

No. Everything here is free and needs no account. Be wary of anyone who asks you to pay before you can start a job.

Where should I look for part-time work in Singapore?

Start with MyCareersFuture and the careers pages of employers you have checked. Our part-time guide lists questions to ask before you say yes.

Do real work-from-home jobs exist in Singapore?

They do. Support, admin, content and many specialist roles are done from home. A genuine employer pays for defined work and does not charge you to begin.

How do people bring in income from home?

Mostly through ordinary jobs done at home: part-time employment, freelance projects or contract roles. What you earn depends on the role, your skills and your hours, and it is never certain.

Is remote work the same as working from home?

Not quite. Working from home means doing agreed hours at your own place. A remote role lets you work from anywhere and may involve an overseas team, so ask which one is on offer.

How can I tell if an online job is genuine?

Find out who pays you, get the terms in writing and walk away from any fee to get started. Legitimate examples include tutoring, moderation and customer support.

Do freelancers get CPF?

Not from clients. A freelancer is self-employed and looks after their own contributions, tax and admin. CPF Board and IRAS publish the current rules.

May I ask my employer for remote or hybrid hours?

Yes. Employees can put in a formal flexible work arrangement request, and employers are expected to consider it fairly under national guidelines. A yes is not assured.

How can I check a recruitment agency?

Look it up in the Employment Agency directory on the MOM website before handing over personal details.
