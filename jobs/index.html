Part-time jobs

Part-time jobs in Singapore

Positions with under 35 contracted hours each week. You are usually an employee on a written contract.

Typical roles

  • Retail and F&B crew
  • Office and admin assistants
  • Tutors and tuition-centre helpers
  • Event and promotion staff
  • Customer service associates

A good fit if

  • Study or family duties limit your time
  • You would like some extra income
  • You are returning to work gradually

Ask before you agree

  • How many hours, and which shifts
  • How and when pay is worked out
  • CPF and leave
  • The notice period

Work from home

Work-from-home roles in Singapore

Jobs carried out at home within agreed hours, often in service and admin. Working from home and freelancing are not the same thing.

Typical roles

  • Chat and email customer support
  • Data entry and back-office admin
  • Virtual assistants
  • Content and social media support
  • Transcription and translation

A good fit if

  • Family means you need to be home
  • You want to cut commuting time and cost
  • You have a quiet corner and dependable internet

Ask before you agree

  • Employee contract or freelance arrangement?
  • Who provides the equipment?
  • Hours and expected reply times
  • How your work is assessed

Remote roles

Remote jobs in Singapore

Roles that don't tie you to one location. Some are with Singapore companies, and others are with overseas teams who want people on Singapore hours.

Typical roles

  • Digital marketing and SEO
  • Graphic and web design
  • Software development and QA
  • Account and project management
  • Recruitment coordination

A good fit if

  • You have a specialist skill
  • You work well unsupervised
  • You write clearly and enjoy it

Ask before you agree

  • Time zone and core hours
  • Who the employer is, and whether a Singapore entity pays you
  • Currency and method of payment
  • How tax and CPF apply

Online work

Online work and working online from home

Work done wholly over the internet, from live teaching to moderation and research. Real online work is paid by a company you can check, for tasks that are clearly defined.

Typical roles

  • Online tutoring
  • Community and chat moderation
  • Online research and data labelling
  • E-commerce listing and order support
  • Virtual event support

A good fit if

  • You like choosing your time slots
  • You are at ease with screen-based work
  • You can stick to a schedule

Ask before you agree

  • Who pays you, and when
  • Any request for equipment or registration fees (a warning sign)
  • What training is given
  • Terms of the trial period

Freelancing

Freelance work in Singapore

Project work where you are self-employed. You choose your clients, and you also deal with your own CPF, tax and admin.

Typical roles

  • Design, illustration and branding
  • Copywriting and translation
  • Video and photo editing
  • Bookkeeping and admin support
  • Web and app development

A good fit if

  • You want to control your own timetable
  • You have a portfolio or a niche skill
  • Uneven income doesn't worry you

Ask before you agree

  • Scope, deadlines and revisions
  • Payment terms and any deposit
  • A written contract for services
  • Your tax responsibilities
Read the full guide
Contract and temp

Contract and temporary jobs

Fixed-term roles that cover a project, a busy season or maternity leave. A practical way to gain recent experience.

Typical roles

  • Admin and operations cover
  • Seasonal retail and logistics
  • Project coordinators
  • Customer service surge teams

A good fit if

  • You like knowing when it ends
  • You are coming back after a break
  • You want to try a firm or industry first

Ask before you agree

  • Length and renewal of the contract
  • Whether an agency employs you
  • Leave and CPF
  • Whether it could become permanent
